Align the Future brand kit

The chosen identity: mark six, the Fraunces wordmark lockup, and the tile icon. All files are in the repository at brand/ on branch claude/align-future-logo-concepts-c4q6h7 of jon-hanover/alignthefuture. This page is the reference for building the homepage.

The mark

The A's left leg sweeps up into the top vector. The A's right leg is the F's stem. The lower vector leaves the A at its crossbar and is the F's middle arm. Both vectors are arcs of one circle, so they share one curvature and differ only in aim.

The lockup

Mark plus the wordmark in Fraunces, with the type set clear of the lower vector. The wordmark is outlines, so it renders identically everywhere without a font.

Icon and favicon

The tile carries the stacked name with FUTURE sized to the same width as ALIGN THE. Below about 64 pixels the text cannot read, so the favicon drops it and keeps the mark.

Palette

Tangerine is an accent and appears once per composition: the lower vector, or FUTURE, or a single button.

Rules

  • Clear space. A margin equal to the height of the A's right leg on all sides of the mark.
  • Minimum size. Mark 24 pixels tall, lockup 140 pixels wide. Smaller than that, use the favicon tile.
  • Backgrounds. Marine and tangerine on paper or white. Bone and tangerine on marine or navy. Never the marine mark on navy.
  • Do not recolor the vectors, add a gradient, rotate the mark, or set the wordmark in another face.
