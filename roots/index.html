Sketches

Root node problems

A root node is the first question in a decision tree. Solve it, and everything above it can branch. Three animations on that idea, after Jesper Lindborg’s work for Google DeepMind, redrawn in Align the Future’s colors. Each one grows a new tree every time it plays.

Breakthrough

A root node pushes up against concrete until it cracks. Everything above grows from that one break: Humanities blue to the left, AI Research gold to the right, and sage green where they meet.

Pathway

A path learns its way through obstacles. The first try wanders down hundreds of dead ends. Each later try remembers the one before and finds the way in fewer steps.

Two roots

Humanities and AI Research grow toward each other underground. When they meet, the trunk breaks through, and the canopy carries both colors.

After Jesper Lindborg’s “Root node problems” for Google DeepMind’s Visualising AI project, as featured in It’s Nice That. These are new drawings, made in code in the browser.
