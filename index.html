Researchers and regulators are losing the race against AI.

On the current trajectory, we risk creating a self-directed superintelligence that cares little for human life or putting a weapon of mass destruction in the hands of anyone with an internet connection.

Even if AI development stalls, and these extinction-level risks do not materialize, we face the prospect today of creating a less human world. Policy makers have just begun to appreciate the damage algorithmic media has done to our cognition, relationships and politics. Today’s chatbots are already capable of furthering those same harms, and today’s agentic models of creating a workplace where humans conform to the needs of the technology rather than the other way around.

We have lost the will to act, to shape our world together.

To young people, in particular, this future seems to be happening to us, not through us.

Our political leaders are hapless, international cooperation seems hopeless, and every day critical decisions about the future of humanity are made by a very small number of people in a lab.

The public is despondent. Some are in denial. Others see no option but to embrace a post-human future or to rage against it.

But it does not have to be this way.

We could confront this threat to humanity together and commit to creating a better life for our children.

We could reach beyond teaching the next generation to “make do” with this future (helping them become better consumers of AI, more productive employees with AI) and instead cultivate the sense of agency and responsibility our generation has lost.

What if we gathered the best minds of the next generation, from across the country and around the world, and prepared them to lead us into an aligned future together?

The concept

An international, experiential and cross-disciplinary youth program.

We propose a program that equips young adults with the knowledge, skills and dispositions required to build AI resilience. By ensuring technical alignment with human values and strengthening democratic and diplomatic processes, we can create a world where they, their children, their children’s children flourish. The program will place graduates directly into labs, think tanks, and government positions where they can make a direct and immediate impact, while raising the level of political discourse and civic engagement for the long haul.

The initial concept is an 8 to 10 week summer program for about 100 rising seniors in high school, hosted by a top university. Students will be mentored by scientists, educators and policy makers at the top of their field.

If successful, this flagship program will be a launchpad for a youth program with broader distribution and an awareness campaign that melts cynicism and helps the public recapture a sense of urgency and agency to build a more human future.
