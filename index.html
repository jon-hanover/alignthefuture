What if we gathered the best minds of the next generation, from across the country and around the world, and prepared them to lead us into an aligned future together?

Researchers and regulators are losing the race against AI.

On the current trajectory, we risk putting a weapon of mass destruction in the hands of anyone with an internet connection, or worse, creating a self-directed superintelligence that cares little for human life.

If AI development stalls, and these extinction-level risks do not materialize, without timely intervention we will create a less human world for our children. Policy makers have begun to appreciate the damage algorithmic media did to our cognition, relationships and politics. Today’s chatbots are already furthering those harms, and deployment of today’s agentic models risks creating a workplace where humans conform to the needs of the technology rather than the other way around.

And at the same time

We have lost the will to act, to shape our world together.

To young people, in particular, this future seems to be happening to us, not through us.

Our political leaders are hapless, international cooperation seems hopeless, and every day critical decisions about the future of humanity are made by a very small number of people in a lab: a scientific subculture with a narrow view of human flourishing, disconnected from the humanities and the general public.

The public is despondent. Some are in denial. Others see no option but to embrace a post-human future or to rage against it.

But it does not have to be this way.

We could confront this threat to humanity together and commit to creating a better life for our children.

We could reach beyond teaching the next generation to “make do” with this future (helping them become better consumers of AI, more productive employees with AI) and instead cultivate the sense of agency and responsibility our generation has lost.

Emerging concept

An international, experiential and cross-disciplinary youth program.

We propose a program that equips young adults with the knowledge, skills and dispositions required to build AI resilience. By ensuring technical alignment with human values and strengthening democratic and diplomatic processes, we can create a world where they, their children, their children’s children flourish. The program will place graduates directly into labs, think tanks, and government positions where they can make a direct and immediate impact, while raising the level of political discourse and civic engagement for the long haul.

The initial concept is an 8 to 10 week summer program for about 100 rising high school seniors, hosted by a top university. Students will be mentored by scientists, educators and policy makers at the top of their field. If successful, this flagship program will be a launchpad for a youth program with broader distribution and an awareness campaign that melts cynicism and helps the public recapture a sense of urgency and agency to build a more human future.
