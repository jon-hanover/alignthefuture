# Working documents

The live state of Align the Future sits in two Google files, not in this repo.
Both change constantly, so this page is a pointer only. It deliberately says
nothing about what is currently in them, and should not need updating as the
work moves.

## Ecosystem Map (Google Sheet)

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ep10_JDoq_SSCxuCIHlIPvWs9X1mfcc_qYowq7e1hGk/edit

Who to reach out to and where each relationship stands. Columns: Priority,
Category, Organization, Key Contact(s), Relationship Owner, Notes, Next Action.
Jon and Aaron both maintain it. Source of truth for the funder, collaborator and
partner pipeline.

## Internal collab doc (Google Doc)

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FWnez43yAabdoFMgFJaaYwiyBpLyf9JWgHECNglRD3U/edit

Tabbed working doc. The tabs hold the internal concept paper, the external copy
bank, and running notes on the last tab.

The concept paper and the copy bank are curated by hand. Leave both alone unless
asked directly. Running notes is the call and meeting log and is safe to append
to.

## Scheduled upkeep

A nightly routine reads Jon's email, Granola notes and calendar and appends to
the Ecosystem Map and to Running Notes. It never touches the concept paper or
the copy bank. A Friday routine emails Jon a digest of what it did that week.

Both need the Google Workspace connector, which is the only path that edits
these files in place.
