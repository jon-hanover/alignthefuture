Align the Future, ribbon monogram

Round two, built on the reference you sent. The kink in the top vector came from two different curves meeting: a steep leg and a flat arrow. Here both arrows are cut from one circle of the same radius, so they share a curvature exactly, and only their aim differs.

What changed from the reference

One arc, two aims. The A's left leg and the top arrow are now a single continuous curve with no change in curvature along its length.

  • The top arrow leaves the A's foot steeply and levels off to about 20° above horizontal by the tip.
  • The bottom arrow is the same arc rotated lower. It finishes just under horizontal, which spreads the two arms enough for the F to read.
  • The stem still tucks under the top arrow and curls into the A's right leg, as in the reference.
  • Variants 2 and 5 aim the bottom arrow lower and higher, so you can pick the spread you like.
Variant 1 · arrows end at −21° and 3°

Ribbon, one arc
