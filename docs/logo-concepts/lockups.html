Align the Future, wordmark lockups

Round four, built on mark six. Ten ways to put the name next to, around, or inside the mark. Lockups 4, 5 and 6 pull the mark's A and F directly into the words. All type is converted to outlines, so what you see is what a printer gets.

Lockup 01

Two lines, mark spans both

Close to your example in structure and nowhere else. ALIGN THE in marine above, FUTURE larger in tangerine below, no box. The mark stands as tall as the two lines together, so the A's foot shares the FUTURE baseline.

Lockup 02

One line, title case

The everyday lockup for a nav bar or an email signature. Jost, with 'the' set light so the two nouns carry the weight. The mark sits a hair taller than the caps so the swoosh has room to clear the ascenders.

Download lockup-02-line.svg
Lockup 03

Stacked, wide and letterspaced

Vertical lockup for avatars, the site's footer, and a slide title. Archivo in its wide cut, tracked out, under a small tangerine rule. The mark gets to be big here.

Download lockup-03-stacked.svg
Lockup 04

The mark is the A of Align

First integration attempt. The mark's A stands in for the capital A, at cap height, and the word continues from its leg. The top vector sweeps up over 'lign'. The lower vector is cut short so it becomes an arrow-tipped crossbar and does not run through the letters.

Viable. The A reads as a letter because it sits on the baseline at cap height with normal spacing after it.
Download lockup-04-integrated-a.svg
Lockup 05

The mark's A and F form both words

Second integration attempt, the ambitious one. ALIGN takes the mark's A. FUTURE takes the mark's F: the same leg, the same swoosh rising from its top, the same short tangerine arm, with the A's left leg left out. Two vectors rise over the two words.

Viable but the F wants a little more presence. The stem leans back, which is the one thing that fights the upright type.
Download lockup-05-integrated-af.svg
Lockup 06

The mark is the F of FUTURE

Third integration attempt. FUTURE is the big word and the whole mark is its F, A included. The top vector rises off the F and points straight at ALIGN THE, set small in tangerine at the top right. The lower vector is the F's middle arm.

Viable and probably the strongest of the three. The arrow pointing at the words does the sentence's job.

Lockup 07

Serif, for the funder deck

A soft, wide serif against the geometric mark. Fraunces at its display size on warm paper. This is the version for a letter, a one-pager, or the cover of the pilot proposal, where the mark should feel considered rather than techy.

Lockup 08

Condensed, one tall line

Bebas Neue, the mark at full cap height. Compact enough for a T-shirt chest print or a banner, and the tangerine FUTURE gives the line a destination.

Lockup 09

The domain is the wordmark

Monospace, lowercase, the URL itself. For the program's technical side: the sandbox, the evals, the GitHub org. Says where to go without a second line.

Badge, reversed

The mark and the name inside a marine tile, bone on blue with the tangerine kept for the vector and FUTURE. App icon, sticker, social avatar, the dark corner of any layout.
