Align the Future, stemless ribbon

Round three. Variant one's geometry with the vertical stem removed: the A's right leg is now the F's stem, and the two vectors are its arms. Ten takes that move the leg, the weight, the arc and the palette. One warm gradient, nine flat inks.

Variant 01

Ink and vermilion

The base case. Right leg leans 18° and carries the F. Near-black ink on warm paper, one vermilion vector. The kind of pairing a letterpress shop would print without asking questions.
