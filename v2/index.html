The challenge

For young people, the future is happening to them, not through them.

Young people are being prepared to adapt to an AI-driven future. To use its tools. To compete in its economy.

At the same time, humanity is losing the race against AI. Speed and capability are outpacing human-centric development and alignment. We are at risk of creating a self-directed superintelligence that cares little for human life. Our politics are broken, international cooperation is lacking, and critical decisions that will impact the next generation are being made by a small number of people in a lab.

But it does not have to be this way.

We could choose to confront this uncertain future together.

We could choose to do more than teach the next generation to “make do” with the AI future. Do more than help them become better consumers of AI. Do more than help them become more productive employees with AI.

We could choose instead to cultivate the sense of agency our generation has lost.

The question should not just be “is AI aligned,” but aligned to what?

We urgently need a more human-centric view of how these systems should be designed and governed.

One grounded in the humanities, moral philosophy, and what makes a good life and a good society.

That is the gap Align the Future exists to fill.

Those entering the fields shaping and governing AI need rigorous knowledge and practical skills, alongside a foundational grounding in human literacy. This is the missing ingredient in creating a world where we can flourish.

Where this can lead

Preparation for consequential work.

We believe the work of aligning AI with human ends will be essential to human flourishing—and foundational to the future economy.

  • Research & engineering

    AI safety research, technical development, and the ongoing work of evaluating and aligning deployed systems.

  • Policy & public service

    Government offices, think tanks, and nonprofits shaping the institutions and rules that govern AI.

  • Entrepreneurship

    New companies building the tools, infrastructure, and services needed for alignment.

  • Public engagement

    Media, advocacy, and civic leadership that help the public understand AI and shape its direction.
