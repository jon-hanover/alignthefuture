The challenge

For young people, the future is happening to them, not through them.

Young people are being prepared to adapt to an AI-driven future. To use its tools. To compete in its economy.

At the same time, humanity is losing the race against AI. Speed and capability are outpacing human-centric development and alignment. We are at risk of creating a self-directed superintelligence that cares little for human life. Our politics are broken, international cooperation is lacking, and critical decisions that will impact the next generation are being made by a small number of people in a lab.

But it does not have to be this way.

We could choose to confront this uncertain future together.

We could choose to do more than teach the next generation to “make do” with the AI future. Do more than help them become better consumers of AI. Do more than help them become more productive employees with AI.

We could choose instead to cultivate the sense of agency our generation has lost.

The question should not just be “is AI aligned,” but aligned to what?

We urgently need a more human-centric view of how these systems should be designed and governed.

One grounded in the humanities, moral philosophy, and what makes a good life and a good society.

That is the gap Align the Future exists to fill.

One where those entering the fields shaping and governing AI are equipped not just with the required knowledge and skills, but the human literacy to create a world where we can flourish.

The Align the Future model

An international, experiential and cross-disciplinary youth program.

We believe transformative AI will arrive quickly. AI capabilities are far outpacing alignment. Preparing young people only to use it is not enough.

We propose a program that equips young minds with the intellectual foundations, practical experience, and relationships to directly enter fields to align AI with human flourishing. By ensuring technical alignment with human values and strengthening democratic and diplomatic processes, we can create a world where they, their children, their children’s children flourish.

The program will place graduates directly into labs, think tanks, and government positions where they can make a direct and immediate impact, while raising the level of political discourse and civic engagement for the long haul.

Pilot model in development for summer 2027. University host and mentor partnerships to be established.

  • 8–10 weeksA summer program, hosted by a top university
  • ~100 studentsRising high school seniors
  • Top mentorsScientists, educators and policy makers at the top of their field
  • Direct impactGraduates placed in labs, think tanks and government positions

Guiding questions

Students would learn with humanities professors, policy makers and AI researchers. Shared inquiry connects technical work to questions of value, responsibility and collective action.

  • Understand humanity

    Humanities

    What makes us human? What does it mean to live a good life? Build a good society? How do humans flourish? Why do we so often falter? What is our full potential as a species?

    Read. Question. Reflect.
  • Shape the future

    Alignment & Governance

    How do we ensure AI serves human ends? What laws do we need? What research do we need? Where is international cooperation most needed and most possible?

    Debate. Coordinate. Govern.
  • Understand AI

    AI Research

    What are the most important, tractable questions? What needs to be solved theoretically? Empirically? What are the biggest open debates and questions?

    Build. Test. Discover.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Open pathways

  4. 04

    Multiply the impact

    Grow an international network that carries this work into institutions.
