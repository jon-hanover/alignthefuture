Who is behind Align the Future?

Align the Future is a growing coalition of educators, entrepreneurs, philanthropic and technology leaders who believe it is not too late for the next generation to take charge of humanity’s future. The effort is being driven by:

Aaron Hoffman

Aaron is a marketing leader with over 15 years of experience at Silicon Valley’s top companies, including Meta and OpenAI. He has built high-impact global teams, and scaled new technology and new categories from zero to billions of users. He now runs a consulting practice that brings marketing strategy, speed, and rigor to purpose-driven organizations, with a diverse network of C-suite allies across tech and nonprofit sectors.

Jon Hanover

Jon is an educator and entrepreneur working with various philanthropic groups to reimagine public education for human flourishing in the age of AI. As a rising high school senior, Jon attended the Research Science Institute (RSI) along with ~100 of the world’s most talented young scientists to conduct research at MIT and build community across boundaries. The structure of RSI is an inspiration for Align the Future.

We are both fathers of young kids and dedicated to ensuring they grow up in a world where they can flourish.
